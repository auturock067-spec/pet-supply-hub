02 / BUYER NOTES

Specifications before samples.

Product decisions depend on intended species, animal size, materials, construction and destination market.

01

Check the intended use

Ask for dimensions, target animal size and load-relevant construction details. A visually similar product may have different specifications.

02

Request model-specific evidence

Identify materials, coatings, cleaning instructions, testing scope and the actual model referenced by each report.

03

Separate claims from checks

Confirm opening dimensions, ventilation, closures and destination-specific requirements before ordering; transport rules vary.

04 / QUALITY & MARKET CHECKS
Evidence for the exact accessory.
Reviewed 29 October 2026. Starting points for ordinary accessories; verify rules for the actual product and destination.
US: identify applicable rules
There is no universal CPSIA certificate for every pet accessory. Identify applicable product rules, materials, intended users and claims; request model-specific test evidence and traceability.
CPSC: testing & certification ↗
EU: consumer-product assessment
Determine GPSR applicability for consumer-intended or foreseeable consumer use. Where applicable, address safety, documentation, traceability and economic-operator details. A B2B shipment is not an automatic exemption.
EU: General Product Safety Regulation ↗
Carriers: check the operating airline
IATA does not certify or approve particular pet containers. Confirm the exact dimensions, animal fit, route, aircraft and travel mode with the operating airline. Generic airline-approved labels are insufficient.
IATA: traveling with pets ↗
For UK, Canada or Australia, verify the exact product against the relevant national rules. No product safety, certification, import clearance or legal advice is provided.
