# PetSupplyHub content and design plan

Reviewed 2026-10-29. Sections: proposition/hero; six illustrated references; search/filter/empty state; sample and quotation guidance; quality/market sources; local RFQ; About; Privacy. Products: collars/leashes, beds, feeding bowls, rope toys, grooming brushes and carriers.

Keep English/Chinese terminology equivalent, including quantity units, no-submission text and safety/claims caveats. Runtime language change also updates title/description and illustration text. Do not translate unverified claims into facts.

Original repository-native SVGs are illustrative construction/reference diagrams, never supplier inventory photographs. No external photos/logos are used. Pet assets are retained from the existing owned repository; hair assets were authored for this project.

Buyer notes should answer a practical next question: dimensions/materials/attachment or use; representative sample; lot/model evidence; packing and destination. Do not expand into food, drugs, treatment claims or unassessed specialist products.

SEO: preview is deliberately noindex,nofollow, with robots disallowing crawl. Public canonical/hreflang/sitemap and indexing are a later acceptance step; do not pretend a private draft is an indexed marketing site.

Maintenance: monthly source/security/link checks; quarterly bilingual terminology and evidence review; change review date only after review. Any supplier, lab, origin or certification assertion needs source and exact-model/lot evidence. Record naming limits; PENG Hair Sourcing remains provisional and is independent of Hairsource Atlanta.
